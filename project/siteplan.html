Site Name

Street Boarder

This name works as a straightforward, easy-to-remember identifier for a site about street skateboarding — it signals the subject immediately to a first-time visitor while still leaving room for history, culture, and technique content beyond just trick tips.

Optional domain availability: streetboarder.com

Site Purpose

The site introduces visitors to the history of skateboarding, highlights the skaters who shaped the sport, and explains the fundamental tricks that make up street skating's basic vocabulary. It's meant as an entry point for people who are curious about skate culture but don't yet know the terminology or timeline.

Scenarios

  • What's the difference between an ollie and a kickflip, and which should I learn first?
  • Who are the skaters most people mention when they talk about skateboarding history?
  • When did skateboarding become an Olympic sport?

Color Schema

Four colors carry the whole site: a near-black for structure, a deep red for accents and emphasis, a curb-paint yellow for highlights, and an off-white concrete tone for backgrounds and body text areas.

Typography

Fraunces — Headings & display
Street Boarder

Used for the site title, section headings, and skater/trick names — a serif with enough character to feel like a deck graphic rather than a generic web font.

Public Sans — Body text
Used for paragraphs, navigation, labels, and all long-form reading text. A clean, highly legible sans-serif that keeps the page easy to read at small sizes.
